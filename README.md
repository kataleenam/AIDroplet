# Final Project for AI for Humans

AI Droplet is tracks your environmental impact while using AI in hopes to deter users from relying too much on AI

**A.	What is the issue you are addressing?**

I wanted to address the environmental and critical thinking impact of AI through my project. The creation and implementation of AI has negatively impacted lots of different areas such as the issue of consent when using data, treating workers as humans, and the state of our world. The reason I chose to focus on the Earth aspect of AI issues is because without a proper planet to live on, the other issues would be obsolete. It is estimated that “training an AI model with the computing power of a human brain for one year is around 126,000 liters of water” (George 93). Considering the fact that there are now thousands of AI models around, this has a pretty substantial hit on this valuable resource. Down to the personal level, a Washington Post and UC study found that “Using OpenAI’s ChatGPT-4 model to generate a 100-word email alone sweats off more than an Evian bottle’s worth of water (519 milliliters)” (Wright). 
 Another factor to consider with my solution is that it pushes towards less human reliance on AI. As talked about in class and through Weston, “Creative people are critical,” I believe creativity is a skill that can be learned and a skill that should be honed with practice (Weston 3). Students relying on AI to complete tasks can be described as people offloading their critical thinking skills to automation. Without the practice of these skills, new and creative ideas/solutions may slowly become obsolete. While that may be an exaggeration, I think it could happen to some extent. I remember us talking in class about how LLM’s generate outputs based on the data they are given. When executed to create something or solve a problem, the voice AI gives is a generic overtone of their data. For example, if you ask AI to generate an email for you, AI makes everyone sound like a middle-aged white man. The only problem is that not everyone is a middle-aged white man. If AI becomes overused, diversity in ideas may decrease. 

**B.	What elements of the course are reflected in the project/paper? (For projects, you should draw on Weston as part of your answer here).**

I drew from the world building aspect of this course to begin to create my project, particularly from Jemisin’s world building workshop. I created my world exactly as the real world but changed one “Element X” (Jemisin). My element X was way in the future where AI had become an increasingly common resource. With this, I had assumed that many of the problems of AI had been somewhat solved (like a better way to outsource data, better labor laws, etc.) and that the environmental aspect of AI had not been. In this future, the overreliance of AI leads to very slow development in all fields and safe drinking water becomes more of a scarce resource that people aim to preserve. 
I really liked my start idea, so I then pulled from Weston to create a solution for this world. I took specifically from Chapter 3 “Multiplying your Options.” In this chapter, Weston provides multiple ways to come up with more creative ideas. My project was a result of “exaggerating.” Weston suggests to “take some feature of the problem and push it as far as it can go” (Weston 26). I thought “What is the best way to solve the problems of AI? Stop using AI.” I initially felt this took things a little too far and would effectively be missing the entire point of this project, but I did like the concept specifically for reducing critical thinking offload. I compared the overuse of AI to the overuse of other common things like alcohol, drugs, social media, etc. Social media stuck with me because the overuse of it was something I personally experienced and what we had just written about in class. My solution to my personal social media addiction was to set timers on my phone to limit my time on the app. This was my inspiration for my project!

**C.	What do you think your project/paper does well to demonstrate that problem and its solution?**

I think the way my project presents the statistics of the problem in a visual way helps make it easier to digest for people of all ages. People get to see in real time how their usage of AI is affecting their planet. Also, the link to the graphic by Business Energy UK helps demonstrate the problem in a multitude of different ways. The solution is suggested with the warning message. Ideally, the warning message would have a timer at the bottom for the next day and say, “24 hours until AI can be used again,” but for demonstration purposes that was left out. Otherwise, the message of the popup is to convince the users to think carefully about continuing to use AI. 8 liters of water is approximately twice the amount of daily intake a human needs. I am relying on this fact to deter users from continuing to generate AI prompts.

**D.	If you had more time, what would you add, change or research further?**

If I had more time, I would make the extension function whenever you send anything to any generative AI model i.e. hit the arrow or ‘enter’ when typing into Chat-GPT, as well as block AI websites when you’ve used the limit for the day. I could also add a lightning bolt alongside the bucket of water to show how much power AI is also taking up when used. I wish I also had more concrete data on how much water is generated for a specific AI prompt and could give more accurate statistics to the user. 
Besides technical fixes, I think I could have also used more world-building to take this project further into the future or shown solutions more creatively. I like my project because it has real world uses, but I think going a different route or adding more UI elements could have helped take this idea to the next level. I wish I could have shown that in this world I created the other problems of AI (labor, data, etc.) were solved, but the overuse of AI to solve problems led to a shortage of new ideas (or something of the sort) and that’s why this application was created. I could have also touched on the world ending/getting closer to the sun, so the resource of water became extremely valuable, and the world is trying to save it in any way they can.
